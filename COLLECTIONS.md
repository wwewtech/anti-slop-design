## 3. Topical & Domain Compilations

### A. The Anti-AI-Slop & Vibe-Coding Cures Pack
Designed specifically to solve the shortcomings of prompt-generated web applications:
- Eliminates the generic dark-mode purple radial gradient.
- Replaces raw emojis with clean 1.5px/2px stroke vector SVGs (Lucide, Phosphor).
- Enforces strict single source of metric truth (no duplicated KPIs).

### B. High-Density SaaS & Enterprise Dashboard Pack
- Unified Page-Level Control Bar (global date range and search).
- Contextual comparison bars and sparklines over vanity 3D globes.
- Separation of frequent operations from deep settings configurations.

### C. 50ms Conversion Landing Page Pack
- Single dominant offer headline with emotional resonance.
- 1 primary conversion CTA + 1 secondary demo link (1-to-3 rule).
- Complete elimination of heavy pre-loaders for instant first contentful paint (FCP).

### D. Tactile Physics & Micro-Interactions Pack
- 0ms instant execution for high-frequency actions (100+/day).
- 120ms–180ms punchy ease-out curves (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`).
- Tactile `:active { transform: scale(0.97) }` press scaling.

### E. WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility & Keyboard Flow Pack
- Unbroken `:focus-visible` ring system without `outline: none` traps.
- Semantic HTML tags and mandatory `aria-label` on icon-only buttons.
- Minimum 44x44px touch targets on mobile viewports.
